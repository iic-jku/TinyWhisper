Definitions and caveats

WNS is the worst negative slack. A positive value means timing is met. Yield is the share of CACE samples whose -3 dB corner is at or above 350 kHz. CACE ran at tt and 27 °C with a 1.2 to 1.8 V supply sweep. Process corners and temperature are commented out in the yaml, so this is not a full PVT sweep. Core power excludes the pads and the analog macro. RF output power is the average of v(vout_RF_filt)² / 1 kΩ over 8 µs of the committed iqmod_top_tb_tran waveform (PEX netlist, tt, 1.5 V, 28 MHz LO), behind the 1 kΩ / 1 kΩ coupled-resonator band-pass. The testbench also measures supply power, but that run was never kept, and no lab measurement exists yet.
