## Provenance and caveats

- **The IHP PDF documents are deliberately not tracked here.** `sg13g2_os_layout_rules.pdf` and `sg13g2_os_process_spec.pdf` are listed in [.gitignore](../../.gitignore), so this folder may hold local copies that are invisible to git. Take them from `$PDK_ROOT/$PDK/libs.doc/doc/` when you need them, which also means they are never stale. The layout rules are the authoritative source when a tool deck and a datasheet disagree.
- **The layout cheatsheet is a convenience, not a rule source.** Its resistance columns assume bulk-aluminium resistivity and come out roughly a factor of two optimistic against the `RESISTANCE RPERSQ` values in `sg13g2_tech.lef`, and its "min. Width" column lists 0.42 um for TopMetal1 and 0.2 um for TopMetal2 where the tech LEF says 1.64 um and 2.0 um (0.42 is the TopVia1 cut size). Take widths from the DRC deck, resistance from the tech LEF or from PEX, and the DC current limits from the tables in the [LibreLane cheatsheet](librelane/librelane_cheatsheet.md), which are read out of the tech LEF. The sheet's `Contact` current limit of 0.3 mA per cut exists only there, the tech LEF has no `DCCURRENTDENSITY` entry for `Cont`.
- **Only the files listed above are tracked.** Other folders may appear here in a working copy (tool cheatsheets, course material, vendor PDFs) and are gitignored.
- **Nothing in `doc/` is generated by a make target.** It is all hand-maintained.
